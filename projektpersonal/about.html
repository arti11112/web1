About AC Milan

Associazione Calcio Milan (Italian pronunciation: [assotʃatˈtsjoːne ˈkaltʃo ˈmiːlan]), commonly referred to as AC Milan or simply Milan (Italian pronunciation: [ˈmiːlan]) mainly outside of Italy,[6] is an Italian professional football club based in Milan, Lombardy. Founded in 1899, the club competes in Serie A, the top tier of Italian football. In its early history, Milan played its home games in different grounds around the city before moving to its current stadium, the San Siro, in 1926.[7] The stadium, which was built by Milan's second chairman, Piero Pirelli, and has been shared with Inter Milan since 1947,[8] is the largest in Italian football, with a total capacity of 75,817.[9] The club has a long-standing rivalry with Inter, with whom they contest the Derby della Madonnina, one of the most followed derbies in football.[10]

Our trophies

AC Milan has a very impressive trophy cabinet and is considered one of the most successful clubs in the history of football. Founded in 1899, the club has enjoyed success both in Italy and in international competitions. In domestic football, AC Milan has won 19 Serie A championships, along with 5 Coppa Italia trophies and 8 Supercoppa Italiana titles. The club has been particularly successful in Europe, winning the European Cup and UEFA Champions League 7 times, including famous victories during the club’s golden eras. Milan has also won the UEFA Super Cup 5 times and the European Cup Winners’ Cup twice. Outside Europe, the club won the Intercontinental Cup 3 times, further demonstrating its success on the world stage. Over the years, many legendary players have helped AC Milan achieve these trophies, contributing to the club’s reputation as one of football’s most decorated and recognizable teams.
