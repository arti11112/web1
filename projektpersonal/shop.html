History of AC Milan Jerseys

AC Milan was founded in 1899, and from the beginning the club became famous for its red-and-black colors, giving the team the nickname Rossoneri. According to club history, red represents fire, while black represents the fear the team wanted to inspire in opponents. Throughout the years, the jersey has changed with different stripe patterns, collars, and designs, but the traditional red and black have always remained. The shirt has been worn by some of Milan’s greatest legends, including Franco Baresi, Paolo Maldini, Kaká, and Andriy Shevchenko. The jersey has also become an important symbol of Milan’s success, representing the club’s history, trophies, and identity. Today, modern AC Milan shirts continue to mix the classic Rossoneri style with new designs and technology.
